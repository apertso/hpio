Choose a currency for each payment

You can pick a currency for both single and recurring payments. If your subscription costs $12 a month, enter the amount, currency, and schedule once. The next payment in the series will use the same currency, and you’ll see the amount in dollars in your payment list, archive, and reminder emails.

If you pay most of your bills in one currency, set it under Settings → General → Default currency. The app will select it for new payments. Available currencies include Russian rubles, US dollars, euros, Georgian lari, Serbian dinars, and Kazakhstani tenge.

Switch the interface to English

On the web or in the Android app, go to Settings → General → Interface language and select English. The payment list, archive, and settings will appear in English, with dates and amounts formatted to match.

Default categories also have English names. For example, «Продукты» appears as “Groceries.” You can give your own categories names in both languages.

Search by amount and update the Android app

  • Search by amount. If you remember paying 1,490 but can’t recall the payment name, enter 1490 in the search box.
  • Update from the app. When a new Android version is available, you’ll see an “Update to …” button next to the current version in General settings. The app downloads the update and opens the Android installer.
